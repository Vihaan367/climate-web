Frequently Asked Questions

What is CIARA?

CIARA is a climate-based parametric insurance platform that helps vulnerable communities receive automatic financial support when climate disaster thresholds are reached.

How does CIARA provide payouts?

CIARA uses predefined climate triggers such as rainfall and flood levels. When these conditions cross the set threshold, emergency funds are automatically released.

How is CIARA different from traditional insurance?

Traditional insurance often requires lengthy claim processes. CIARA enables faster response through automatic, data-based payouts.

Who can use CIARA?

CIARA is designed for communities that are vulnerable to climate risks and need faster financial protection during disasters.
